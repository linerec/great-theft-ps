## Sony application credentials

`src/console/psn-account.ts` contains the OAuth client id and client secret of Sony's own Remote Play application, exactly
as chiaki-ng publishes them. They are not issued to this project. They are used only by the optional "get my PSN account id"
step, only on the bridge, and only for the account of the person running it. Using them may conflict with Sony's terms of
service; that risk is the user's to accept, which is why the step shows a notice and needs a confirmation. If Sony asks for
them to be removed, remove them: the rest of the project works without them (the account id can be typed in).
